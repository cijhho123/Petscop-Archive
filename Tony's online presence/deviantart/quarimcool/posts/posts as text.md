Then there are the 'smart' people. These people can fit in the talented, middle, or untalented groups. The smart people think that everyone should just shut up and draw.

Everyone starts out as an untalented person. They constantly get mocked by the higher groups. The ones that use that as motivation to improve later become a middle person. The ones that become angry stay untalented.

Once you have reached the middle state, you are usually satisfied. The ones that really want to improve further do so. They become talented people.
