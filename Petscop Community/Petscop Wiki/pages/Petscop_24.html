Synopsis

The player opens the pause menu. The game preview window displays the Newmaker Plane with blue lighting similar to how it appeared near the end of Petscop 23. The message under the piece counter reads "Now:". The Book of Baby Names is selected and, unlike in Petscop 6, the player is allowed to use this option.

A list of names, alongside very short descriptions, scrolls across the screen. After this, a note from Anna appears, directed at Michael Hammond whose 7th birthday was celebrated.
