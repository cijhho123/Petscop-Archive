Welcome to the Petscop Wiki

The Petscop Wiki is a community-edited encyclopedia for documenting information on the Petscop webseries, along with relevant findings and credible theories.

Petscop is a webseries telling a story about a mysterious PlayStation game of the same name. The videos are presented in a Let's Play format, posted on YouTube on a channel titled "Petscop", narrated by a person named Paul. For more information about the Petscop series, see Petscop.
