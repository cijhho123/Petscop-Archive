Petscop Discovery Pages

Namespace: Articles · Revision 10359 · view live · wikitext source

Petscop Discovery Pages is the name of a website mentioned several times throughout Petscop. The website is first mentioned in Petscop 1 on the note that came with the game, and it's partially shown on a Tarnacop machine in the Garage during Petscop 14.

The specific contents of the website are largely unknown, however, Petscop 14 shows that the site includes various information about and screenshots of Petscop, along with a page titled "Your Child" (according to Paul) which contains a large amount of text.
