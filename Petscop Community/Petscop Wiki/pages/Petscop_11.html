Synopsis

Paul is following a map he drew out to look for something in the game. He encounters the tire and asks it where the house is, to be directed to a road and then the house. The key he has from the party room unlocks the door to the house, but he can't open it; after a while, the door opens itself. Paul enters the house for the first time.

He explores the house with its various details and textboxes popping up. Interactive calendars on the wall appear to signify that it is currently December 25th. After entering the bathroom of the house, a cutscene plays prior to fully entering. Paul encounters a symbol block above the bathtub, which can be walked into with a ramp. The video cuts once he is about to touch the symbol block.

The video changes to footage of the title screen. The title screen transitions to playing a recording via demo playback, which features the tunnel near the grave room. This appears to play a cutscene of the camera moving along the road. After it reaches a certain point (multiple video cuts later), the game then transitions to the Newmaker Plane, with the guardian and Marvin standing in front of a bench. An image reading P2 to TALK appears at the top of the screen. Marvin and the player begin communicating using this feature. Marvin refers to the player as "Pall." They then enter the school. As the player is exploring the school, the video cuts back to the house.

With the video returned to Paul's gameplay, Paul enters the bathroom again, identically to how he did previously, however the symbol block and bathtub ramp are not there anymore. Paul mentions that he thought he saw the symbol block. After exiting the bathroom, various aspects of the house have changed. The calendars indicate it is now June 5th, and a note from someone is pinned on the wall, telling the reader that her husband may come to the house and asking the reader to stay overnight. Paul finds Care's bedroom and Care A on the bed, which cannot be directly caught. At one point, Paul enters a closet in the room, and the door shuts, preventing him from leaving. When the clock reaches 6:15, Marvin appears from the window, and takes Care. After this, the room resets back to normal, and Care is back on the bed. Paul then goes outside to find a ladder outside of Care's window, which he uses to enter like Marvin did, and catches Care A. After Care A's description says to "check your bathroom now," Paul returns to the bathroom, but doesn't find anything.

The video then cuts again to another recording (or possibly the same recording at a different point) being played. In the recording, the player navigates to a classroom in the school, where they find Marvin, who instructs them to play music. After this, the Needles Piano appears. The player picks it up, and begins playing. Eventually, the notes turn red, and Marvin asks the player to stop. After this, the video ends.

Theories

The green dates on the calendar are possibly days Marvin is with Care. Jan 1 - June 1 are the days he has custody of her, then the almost solid six months between June 5 - Nov 10 she spends with him after he kidnaps her.
