Petscop 6 is the sixth video in the Petscop series, it was uploaded to the Petscop Youtube channel on April 20, 2017.

In this episode, Paul leaves the game idle for several hours while viewing the windmill (as the red tool had suggested prior), and Marvin appears and writes a message.

A transcript for this episode can be accessed at Petscop 6/Transcript.

Synopsis

The video starts out with a shot of the windmill, the screen cuts to black and the words "2 hrs 39 mins later" are shown briefly before the video cuts back to the windmill. Marvin can be seen emerging from the right side of the screen as a shadow monster man, and he walks behind the windmill. The windmill begins spinning in the opposite direction, and Marvin approaches the camera.

The camera then rotates, and Marvin writes a message. After this, the camera turns back to it's original rotation.

The video then cuts to a message reading "1 hr 24 mins later" and the windmill vanishes.

After this, the video cuts to Paul in the tool room. He walks to the surface of the Newmaker Plane and finds the camera has turned red and descended in height; Paul notes that he has seen the camera at this height before.

Paul heads away from the camera (in the opposite direction of the windmill) and finds the location Marvin used to write the message, as well as Toneth, who can be captured. Paul views Toneth's description in the pet menu, as well as showing Care's presence in the pet menu and the non-functioning "Book of Baby Names" button. Paul explores the area with the windmill foundation, but it appears unchanged from its showcasing in Petscop 4.

Paul returns to the tool room and repeats Marvin's questions to the red tool.

The video cuts to Paul in the grave room, where he discusses how he believes the game is trying to make it seem like there is an "entity" within it, and that he had created another save file, in which no strange occurrences have happened.

Notes

  • Due to a YouTube bug, the video thumbnail displays an incorrect duration. Fans sometimes speculate that this was intentional, or that it implies additional footage was edited out, but these claims are unfounded.
