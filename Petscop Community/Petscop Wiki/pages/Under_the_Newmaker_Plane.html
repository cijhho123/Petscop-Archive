Under the Newmaker Plane

Namespace: Articles · Revision 9868 · view live · wikitext source

The area under the Newmaker Plane consists of rooms and pathways with various purposes and gameplay features. It can be accessed from cellar doors on the Newmaker Plane.

Rooms & locations

Cellar

The cellar is the first location Paul encounters under the Newmaker Plane, in Petscop 2. It is the room that connects to the first cellar door that Paul finds.

Office

Main article: Office

The office comes after a hallway between it and the cellar. It contains a ringing phone that reads "Care left the room", and an interactive note depicting the A/B/NLM stages of rebirthing.

Hallway

The hallway between the office and tunnel (in Room Impulse as "hallway2") contains three images of "places" that can be interacted with to view closer. The images depict the house, school, and windmill.

Tunnel

The tunnel area prominently features a tunnel which cars drive out of. It leads to the grave room and the area outside the Child Library. Marvin appears here at one point.

Grave room

Main article: Grave room

The grave room is named for it being the location of Michael Hammond's grave. It also contains a shed with two floors that involve a flower and Care NLM, related to catching Care NLM as a pet.

Tool room

Main article: Tool room

The tool room is where the red tool (and pink tool) are located, along with a screen that allows players to watch the windmill.

Quitter's Room

Main article: Quitter's Room

The Quitter's Room is a room with two mirrored sides to it, where another player typically mirrors the current player. Tiara is initially found here, and one of the sides is closed off at first.

Child Library

Main article: Child Library

The Child Library contains an interactive easel to input face details into as to pull up a room related to that face after some time. Certain rooms based on faces shown in other areas (such as the grave room) seem to lead to rooms that have more significance. In one case, one room leads to the party room.

Outside the Child Library leading from the tunnel room to it is a long hallway with text on the wall reading "GOOD GRIEF AND ALAS". The side opposite has a doorway that leads to an empty area with no purpose.

Party room

Main article: Party room

The party room is accessed through Lina Leskowitz's room in the Child Library. It contains multiple interactive presents with various objects, including the green key to the house, and is where Odd Care can be accessed.
