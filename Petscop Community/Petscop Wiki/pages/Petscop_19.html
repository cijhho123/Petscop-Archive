Synopsis

The video starts in the secret menu. The player selects "All Recordings" and scrolls through the entire list of 248 recordings, until they reach the end of the list.

The oldest recording, sort-test, is selected. Its gen is 1. The player selects "play." The guardian has no animation and also uses an early concept from Petscop 18. The room consists of a long row of tiles, using textures that also appear in Even Care. The player moves through each of the tiles, presumably to test depth-sorting.

The video cuts, and a gen 2 recording, titled mike2, is played. The player (presumably Mike) appears in an unfinished version of Even Care. After moving through walls, they enter Randice & Wavey's room, finding an Easter Egg on the right side of the room.

The screen then cuts to a gen 3 recording named care, and then another, named belle. Numerous additional recordings from gen 4 and 5 are shown, ending with one called belle3, where the player (presumably Belle) catches Pen.
