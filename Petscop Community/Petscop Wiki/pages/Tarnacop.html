Tarnacop

Namespace: Articles · Revision 10202 · view live · wikitext source

Tarnacop machines are computers that are seen several times throughout the series. Very little is known about them, however they seem to be closely tied to Garalina and the development of Petscop.

History

A Tarnacop machine is first seen in the garage during Petscop 14. When Paul interacts with it, it displays low resolution images of the Petscop Discovery Pages, along with an image of the guardian standing in the Newmaker Plane. The machine appears to be running Internet Explorer 5 under Windows 9x.

During Petscop 23, when the player explores the school's third floor, eight Tarnacop machines can be found in front the numbered doors. Each of them are displaying the Garalina logo, rotating counter-clockwise, except for the seventh one, which is displaying an image of the windmill interior. After the player uses the green tool to collect the pieces in the room, all of the Tarnacop machines appear to lose power.

Later in Petscop 23, the Tarnacop machine from the garage can be seen in a loading screen.

The Petscop Soundtrack's description states that the tracks were "Recovered from a Tarnacop machine."
