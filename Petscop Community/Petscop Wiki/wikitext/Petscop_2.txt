'''Petscop 2''' is the second video uploaded to the Petscop account. It was uploaded on April 1, 2017.

In this episode, [[Paul]] explores numerous new locations under the [[Newmaker Plane]].

''A transcript for this episode can be accessed at [[{{PAGENAME}}/Transcript]].''
==Synopsis==
Petscop 2 begins at the same location that [[Petscop 1]] ended at, on the Newmaker Plane. Several seconds into the video, the door the [[guardian]] is standing by opens, accompanied by a short tune. The video cuts to black briefly until [[Paul]] returns, explaining that he left the room and came back to find the door open. Paul enters the door, leading to areas [[under the Newmaker Plane]]. 

Paul explores this region, including finding depictions of [[Care]] in the [[office]], interactive images on the walls (of the [[house]], [[school]], and [[windmill]]), a tunnel with cars, the [[grave room]] with [[Michael Hammond]]'s grave and an unobtainable [[Care NLM]], the [[tool room]] and [[red tool]], and [[Tiara]] and the [[Quitter's Room]]. At one point in the Quitter's Room, the movements of Tiara become desynchronized from Paul's as the same tune that played when the door opened at the beginning of the video. The video cuts as Paul reviews the footage of what happened.

Paul explores further, and finds a staircase that leads to another section of the Newmaker Plane. The video then ends.
