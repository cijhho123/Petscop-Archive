This is a list of '''presumed''' mistakes, errors, or inaccuracies in [[Petscop]].

== List ==
=== P2 to TALK ===
There are some strange examples of phoneme use in [[P2 to TALK]].
*"Put" in Petscop 23 only includes phonetics that suffice for "ut", not including "p" (R2 + SQUARE). 
*"House" in Petscop Soundtrack only includes phonetics that include "ouse", not including "hh" (L2 + DOWN).
*"Music" (Petscop 11) uses R1 + CIRCLE + R2 + CROSS for the "u" sound rather than L2 + UP ("y" sound).

=== Startup ===
The PlayStation startup used at the beginning of Petscop 1 is inaccurate (possibly custom-made), as the SCEA fade-in is too slow.

=== Color limitations ===
The PlayStation generally only renders 16-bit colors, using dithering to reduce banding artifacts. While Petscop does apply dithering, the colors appear to use a higher bit depth than what the PlayStation is capable of rendering.

=== Fonts ===
Poetsen One is a font used in numerous instances in Petscop, including the title screen logo, most surface game [[loading screen]]s, 3D signs, and the "GiRL" text on the [[School#Second_floor|school's second floor]]. The font itself seems to have been published in 2012 according to its copyright date embedded within the font file<ref>Creator's official download of Poetsen One: http://www.rfuenzalida.com/poetsen/</ref>.

The font used in the [[Garalina]] logo, Days Sans Black, has copyright attributed to 2014<ref>Official download: https://www.1001fonts.com/days-sans-black-font.html</ref>.
