'''Keys''' are objects that the [[Player character|player]] can collect, which can be used to unlock doors to enter new areas. 

Keys hover above the player, in which they rotate. They shrink and move toward the door when they are used. 

==Instances==
===House key===
[[File:Key_house.png|thumb|75px|The house key]]
The house key is a large green key that is collected out of the right present in the [[party room]]. 

It is used to unlock the [[house]], however it does not necessarily open the house door.
{{clear}}
===Garage key===
[[File:Key_garage.png|thumb|75px|The garage key]]
The garage key is a small silver key that is collected by painting over the wall in the [[master bedroom]] with black paint from the [[bucket]]. This is only available through [[recording]] manipulation. 

It is used to unlock the [[garage]]. However, entering the garage in the recording in which the key must be obtained results in the [[Strange situation]] event.
