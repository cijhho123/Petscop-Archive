'''Petscop 8''' was uploaded May 9, 2017 with a video length of 3:59.

In this episode, [[Marvin]] appears unexpectedly, and [[Paul]] attempts to follow him.

''A transcript for this episode can be accessed at [[{{PAGENAME}}/Transcript]].''

== Synopsis ==
The video begins with [[Paul]] in front of the underpass, walking back and forth in front of the road..

[[Marvin]] appears from the tunnel. Bewildered, Paul follows him to the hallway where Marvin gestures to the pictures of the [[school]] and [[house]]. After seeing the pictures, Paul realizes this must be Marvin. Marvin then runs into a wall, again gesturing to Paul. Not understanding the meaning, Paul thinks Marvin is confused. Marvin, then begins walking North and levitates upwards, disappearing.

Paul tries to follow Marvin and climbs up to the [[Newmaker Plane]]. He wanders around but can't find anything.

The video cuts and Paul is talking to someone back where Marvin ran into the wall:

<blockquote>
Yeah, well, the thing is, it's not.. here now. There's not even a passageway or anything.<br /> 
...Can you remember when you saw this?<br /> 
...Well, it could've changed between then and 2004. After 2004, my mom had it, reportedly.<br /> 
...Uh... Well, okay. Tha–
</blockquote>

The end of the phone call is cut off, perhaps to avoid saying the person's name.
