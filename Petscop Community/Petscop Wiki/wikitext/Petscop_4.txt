'''"Petscop 4"''' is the fourth video uploaded to the [[Petscop (channel)|Petscop channel]]. It was uploaded on April 7, 2017.

In this episode, [[Paul]] discusses his findings of a camera in the [[Newmaker Plane]], and the [[windmill]].

''A transcript for this episode can be accessed at [[{{PAGENAME}}/Transcript]].''

== Synopsis ==
Paul returns to the staircase he found at the end of [[Petscop 2]] and enters the Newmaker Plane. After walking left for several seconds, he finds a dark object protruding from the ground, which he speculates is a camera, corresponding to the screen in the [[tool room]].

Paul walks forward to where the [[windmill]] should be located, only to find a transparent circle in it's place.
