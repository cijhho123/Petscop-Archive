'''Daniel''' is a character mentioned in [[Petscop 17]] and [[Petscop 19]]. He is [[Care]]'s cousin.

In Petscop 19, during the "[[mike]]2" [[recording]] set in [[gen]] 2 of [[Even Care]], collecting the [[egg]] mentions Daniel:
<blockquote>
What? One of the eggs... was in Daniel’s game??<br>
What an unfair trick!<br>
Have you found every egg in the office?
</blockquote>

==Theories==
Daniel may be [[Rainer]]'s real name, considering the in-game textbox referring to [[Petscop (game)|Petscop]] presumably as "Daniel's game".
