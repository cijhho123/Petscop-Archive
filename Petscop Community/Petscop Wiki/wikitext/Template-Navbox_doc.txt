;Description
:This template is used to create a basic navigation box.
;Syntax
:Type <code>{{t|navbox|...}}</code> somewhere, with parameters as shown below.
;Sample output

<pre>{{navbox
|header=Land of Bob
|body=This <nowiki>[[place]]</nowiki> and that <nowiki>[[place]]</nowiki>.
}}</pre>

Results in...

{{navbox
|header=Land of Bob
|body=This <nowiki>[[place]]</nowiki> and that <nowiki>[[place]]</nowiki>.
}}
