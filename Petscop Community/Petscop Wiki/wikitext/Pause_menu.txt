[[File:Pause menu.png|thumb|The pause menu]]
The '''pause menu''' in [[Petscop (game)|Petscop]] has various options and displays the amount of [[pieces]] a player has along with a message.

== Selections ==
* '''Resume Game:''' Unpauses the game. (This can also be done with the Start button.)
* '''[[Options]]:''' Changes the game's settings such as the volume, graphics, controls, and the [[sound test]].
* '''[[Pets]]:''' View the list of pets and their descriptions.
* '''[[Book of Baby Names]]:''' Brings up the "Petscop Kids" Credits Section. ([[Paul]] stated that this selection doesn't do anything, although in [[Petscop 24]] it shows this "credits" section.)
* '''Quit Game:''' Allows the player to save and quit the game. ([[Recording]] playback typically does not show this option.)

=== Secret Menu ===
{{main|Secret menu}}
In the [[Sound Test]] menu of the Options section, playing the correct sounds in a specific order will cause the player to access a [[secret menu]] that allows the use of direct recording playback, [[Room Impulse]], and images shown in "[[Extra Stuff]]".

== Messages ==
Various messages are shown in the top right under the pieces count at different points in the game.

* Have you found anything weird lately? There is much to find in this place. ''(Petscop 6)''
* Take them home! ''(Petscop 7)''
* You're in the other place too! ''(Petscop 9)''
* The pets really seem to like you. Thanks for playing Petscop. ''(Petscop 9)''
* I'll come out in a minute. Keep playing. ''(Petscop 11)''
* Demo Recording ''(during demo playback of recordings)''
* The ghost room is a ship in a bottle. ''(Petscop 17)''
* Everything is a trick. ''(Petscop 19)''
* There are no changes, only replacements. ''(Petscop 19)''
* Recording Playback ''(during recording playback via the secret menu)''
* There is so much to discover in the world of Petscop. ''(Petscop 20)''
* Your butt leaves a cavity in the chair. ''(Petscop 20)''
* Now: ''(Petscop 24)''
