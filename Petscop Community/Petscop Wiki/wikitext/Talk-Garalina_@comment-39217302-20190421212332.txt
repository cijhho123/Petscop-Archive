One Petscop theorist on YouTube divided the word "Garalina" into "gara" and "lina". He compared "gara" to the Latin "garum", a type of fish, and "lina" was similar to "line", although not a direct translation. The Garalina logo resembles a vertical line. The white space inside the G resembles a hook. The extended body of the G has a shape similar to a sinker used to fish with.
