'''Petscop 24''' was uploaded at midnight (UTC-4) on September 2, 2019.

''A transcript for this episode can be accessed at [[{{PAGENAME}}/Transcript]].''

==Synopsis==
The player opens the [[pause menu]]. The game preview window displays the [[Newmaker Plane]] with blue lighting similar to how it appeared near the end of [[Petscop 23]]. The message under the [[Pieces|piece]] counter reads "Now:". The [[Book of Baby Names]] is selected and, unlike in [[Petscop 6]], the player is allowed to use this option.

A list of names, alongside very short descriptions, scrolls across the screen. After this, a note from [[Anna]] appears, directed at [[Michael Hammond]] whose 7th birthday was celebrated.
