I have been meaning to make a blog on this, our admins, [[User:Petscopinvestigator|Petscopinvestigator]], [[User:Camwood777|Camwood777]], and the wiki's founder [[User:Dreamish|Dreamish]] have all been inactive for well over 90 days, and as a result are considered to be inactive. Without any admins, we are vulnerable to spam, vandalism, trolling, flaming, etc. and we have no way to expel vandals and other such individuals that may damage our wiki and drive away potential contributors. Valiant efforts have been made by contributers such as [[User:Guywholikesediting|Guywholikesediting]] to undo vandal edits, but an admin is necessary to do so efficiently and ban the vandal users. As per the [http://community.wikia.com/wiki/Adoption:Requests guidelines] of Wikia central, we should, as a community, have a discussion on who to appoint as bureaucrat to manage the wiki from now on, unless one of the other admins returns.


I personally think we need an admin who will get rid of all the gamejacking attempts the wikia unfortunately undergoes, such as [http://petscop.wikia.com/wiki/User_blog:PaulPetscop/Hey!_My_name%27s_paul this one], [http://petscop.wikia.com/wiki/Censorship#comm-1120 this], etc. I personally would be happy to do it, I have experience as admin on [http://historyfiction.wikia.com/wiki/Special:ListUsers/sysop Infinite Histories Wiki] and [http://minecraftfanon.wikia.com/wiki/Special:ListUsers/sysop Minecraft Fanon Wiki], but really it just matters whether or not it gets done moreso than who does it.

Note that any potential admins must have a wikia/fandom account and should probably be active contributers on the wiki.
