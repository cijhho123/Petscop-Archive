[[Tarnacop]] machines are computers that are seen several times throughout [[Petscop|the series]]. Very little is known about them, however they seem to be closely tied to [[Garalina]] and the development of [[Petscop_(game)|Petscop]].

== History ==
A Tarnacop machine is first seen in the [[garage]] during [[Petscop 14]]. When [[Paul]] interacts with it, it displays low resolution images of the [[Petscop Discovery Pages]], along with an image of the [[guardian]] standing in the [[Newmaker Plane]]. The machine appears to be running [[wikipedia:Internet Explorer 5|Internet Explorer 5]] under [[wikipedia:Windows 9x|Windows 9x]].

During [[Petscop 23]], when the player explores the [[School#Third_floor|school's third floor]], eight Tarnacop machines can be found in front the numbered doors. Each of them are displaying the Garalina logo, rotating counter-clockwise, except for the seventh one, which is displaying an image of the [[windmill]] interior. After the player uses the [[green tool]] to collect the [[pieces]] in the room, all of the Tarnacop machines appear to lose power.

Later in Petscop 23, the Tarnacop machine from the garage can be seen in a [[loading screen]].

The [[Petscop Soundtrack]]'s description states that the tracks were "Recovered from a Tarnacop machine."
