'''"Petscop 5"''' is the fifth video uploaded. It was uploaded on April 11, 2017.

In this episode, [[Paul]] attempts to investigate the [[red tool]], but this is interrupted when it unexpectedly changes color and starts responding abnormally.

''A transcript for this episode can be accessed at [[{{PAGENAME}}/Transcript]].''

== Synopsis ==
Paul asks the red [[tool]] a number of questions which he had found to give unique responses when playing the game off-camera. The red tool abruptly changes from its usual pale red color to bright pink, surprising Paul.

When asked "Who are you?" the pink tool responds with "TURN OFF PLAYSTATION". Paul asks "Why?", to which it replies "[[Marvin|MARVIN]] PICKS UP TOOL HURTS ME WHEN PLAYSTATION ON". These answers differ from the ones given when the tool is in its red form. Additionally, they are in block capitals instead of a handwritten font and take much longer to appear, depending on the length of the message.

Before Paul can respond, the tool reverts to its red color and gives ordinary answers as it did previously.
