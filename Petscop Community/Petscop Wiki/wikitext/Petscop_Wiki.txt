==Welcome to the Petscop Wiki==
The '''Petscop Wiki''' is a community-edited encyclopedia for documenting information on the ''Petscop'' webseries, along with relevant findings and credible theories.

'''Petscop''' is a webseries telling a story about a mysterious PlayStation game of the same name. The videos are presented in a [[wikipedia:Let's Play|Let's Play]] format, posted on YouTube on [https://www.youtube.com/c/Petscop a channel titled "Petscop"], narrated by a person named Paul. ''For more information about the Petscop series, see [[Petscop]].''
