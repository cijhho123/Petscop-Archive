==Synopsis==

The video begins in the garage, with an empty text box visible on screen, like in the previous video.

It is unclear whether Paul is the one controlling the game. The video does not contain any indication that it's a recording at this point in time, and the video contains no narration from Paul.

The [[pause menu]] is opened. "The ghost room is a ship in the bottle" is displayed under the piece counter.

The player navigates to [[Sound Test]] and enters an esoteric code in order to access the [[secret menu]]. From here, the player chooses [[Room Impulse]], selects the [[house]], then sets the "[[Gen]]." field to 10. Upon pressing "Play," the player is taken back to the game screen where the birthday variation of the house is loaded in.

A large number of players appear to be walking around the room. They each appear as [[guardian|guardians]], but with red triangular heads, identical to how [[Paul]] looked during [[Petscop 12]]. After using a dial on the lower left corner of the screen to focus numerous guardians, the player eventually selects one which leaves the house.

Outside of the house, the movements of each guardian are reversed. The player focuses the same same guardian that was selected previously. Shortly afterwards, a text box appears, telling the reader that they are [[Carrie Mark]], and inquiring about what happened on November 11th of 1997, asking the player to retrace their steps.

While traversing the [[Newmaker Plane]], the player finds [[Lina Leskowitz]]'s grave.

Eventually, the player reaches the underground road, and begins walking North. The video then ends.
