'''Petscop Soundtrack '''was uploaded on November 28, 2019.

The video functions as a finale for [[Petscop]], along with providing a soundtrack.

The description is mostly written in an in-universe perspective, describing the tracks being obtained from a "[[Tarnacop]] machine", though it also links to a resource to buy a high quality version of the soundtrack.

''A transcript for this episode can be accessed at [[{{PAGENAME}}/Transcript]].''

== Synopsis ==
The majority of the video consists of timestamped music tracks and sections of compiled sound effects.

At 42:03 in the video until the end, an in-game session of [[Tiara]] talking to "[[Pall]]" plays out through [[P2 to TALK]].

== Notes ==
* The area shown as the track ''level2'' is played resembles the [[loading screen]] that appears during [[Petscop 20]] as the player exited [[Even Care]].
* Some of the sound effects in ''SFX1'' and ''SFX2'' are [[Unused_content#Sound_Effects|not used]] anywhere else in the series.
* Most of the SFX sections appear to play in the order of presentation in the [[Sound Test]] menu. This is not exact, as some entries are not in order.
* The [https://petscop.bandcamp.com/album/petscop-soundtrack Bandcamp] page for the soundtrack lists its artist as "[[Rainer]]".
* The word "Boss" in the gamepad language is displayed when the phonetic combination comes out as "[[Lina Leskowitz|Lina]]".
* The thumbnail image appears to be the white sphere from the Sound Test superimposed on a pink background, which is in turn reminiscent of a [[loading screen]] with a black disc from [[Petscop 6]]. This image is also used for the Bandcamp album art, although cut in half to fit a square.
* The bench and window in the final segment appears to be an exact mirror of the bench seen through a window in [[Petscop 9]], indicating the two locations are on the opposite sides of the same wall.

== Theories ==
While the track ''bathroom-tomb'' is not heard elsewhere in the series, it is the subject of several theories:
* It has been speculated that [[Rainer]] committed suicide in the [[House#Bathroom|house's bathroom]].
* As the track is played in the soundtrack video, the camera is focused on the dirt column in Randice & Wavey's room, emphasizing the similarities with this room's layout and that of the house.
