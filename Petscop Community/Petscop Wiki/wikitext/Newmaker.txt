'''"Newmaker"''' is a title referring to select people. [[Rainer]] calls himself a Newmaker, and the [[red tool]]'s response to the player asking “Who am I?” is “Newmaker”. 

According to [[Care NLM]]’s description, the Newmaker can "close the loop" by turning Care NLM into [[Care A]]. Other intended purposes of the Newmaker are currently unknown. 

The [[Newmaker Plane]], the base location of the hidden sections of [[Petscop (game)|Petscop]], also seem to refer to the Newmaker. 

In reference to the player being called a Newmaker by the red tool, along with Care NLM's description, the title presumably is either being given to the current player ([[Paul]]), or a secondary intended player.

== Notes ==
"Newmaker" was [[Candace Newmaker allusions|a reference to the story of Candace Newmaker]], but the [[Tony|creator of the series]] stopped using it, as he felt that it was inappropriate to reference such a thing in a fictional series like Petscop. The changes he made were relatively minor however, as he "was never gonna go much deeper with the references."<ref>https://egmnow.com/theres-something-hiding-in-petscop/</ref>
