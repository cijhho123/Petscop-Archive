After attending a full screening of Petscop at Spectacle Theater in Brooklyn, I realized that many YouTubers have fundamentally misinterpreted what this enigmatic series is truly about. In this video, I break down the hidden layers of Petscop that mainstream analyses have completely overlooked or misunderstood.

Considering Petscop’s subtle critique of neoliberalism, austerity politics, and systemic exploitation, it’s abundantly clear to me that most popular interpretations of this complex work miss the mark. Discover the deeper subtext of Petscop that so many YouTubers seem to have glossed over. No shade! (Well, maybe just a little. Lmao.)

Join me as we uncover the real story behind Petscop, challenge popular conceptions of the work, and explore why a multidisciplinary approach is essential to grasp its nuances and complexities. Don’t let the common misconceptions fool you—Petscop is far more profound than it appears. Together, let’s dive into what makes Petscop not just a creepy web series, but a haunting reflection of our socio-economic landscape in the 21st century.
