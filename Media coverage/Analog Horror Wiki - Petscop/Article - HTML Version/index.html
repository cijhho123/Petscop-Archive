Petscop

First released

March 12th 2017 - September 2nd 2019

Episodes

1 - 24

Network

YouTube

Created by

Tony Domenico (Youtube channel: Petscop)

Petscop is not a analog horror letsplay series on YouTube about an old PlayStation game made in the late 1990's simply titled "Petscop" that the protagonist named Paul found. Petscop doesn’t classify as analog horror because both formats, being a PlayStation game and a letsplay series are digital media. Simply put, analog horror is something about ANALOG MEDIA (like VHS or ANALOG TV). Petscop is a webseries about a fake game.

Paul has been telling people in the past about this game but nobody believed him, so he decided to start documenting the game right from the start. He starts telling the viewer how the game works and how to play through all the levels, Paul reads a note that came with the game apparently telling the reader to press a couple of buttons and discovers a secret side to Petscop.
As Paul continues to play, the more dark and creepy secrets he finds within the game, Paul soon discovers that the game may be linked to his own past...

The series ran from *March 12th 2017* to *September 2nd 2019* there are 24 episodes in the series each of them digging deeper and deeper into the game's core. Things such as puzzles, games, prizes can unlock mysteries that are waiting to be solved. The series has many creepypasta like elements and brings a Ben Drowned [are you insane? do you smoke crack? is that what you do?] feeling to the series and an unnerving vibe/tone it brings.
